1Goalbud — first pass

What I assumed. Friendly-chunky: Figtree 700–900, 24–28px radii, flat fills, no gradients. Sage #3F6F52 is the app accent and the first of eight goal colours. Warm off-white ground so completed states can be a tint rather than a badge. Icons are placeholder glyphs — the real ~30 get drawn later.

How the no-shame rules landed in pixels. Nothing undone is ever coloured: empty circles, no counts of what is missing. Weekly goals say “2 this week”, never “2 of 3”. Skipped renders as a grey dash and a kind line. There is no red anywhere in the palette, and no progress bar that can be read as “short of target”.

Open questions. Whether Today should group Done into a collapsed footer (shown in 1b), and whether skip lives behind a ⋯ or sits in the open.
